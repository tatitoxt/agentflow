# Billing (synthetic demo data)

Acme Notes is a fictional product used only for AgentFlow demos.

Acme Notes bills monthly on the day the subscription started. Invoices are emailed to the
account owner and can be downloaded from Settings > Billing.

Refunds for duplicate or incorrect charges are processed within 5 business days of approval.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Annual plans can be cancelled within 14 days of renewal for a prorated refund.
